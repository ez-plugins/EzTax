[CENTER][SIZE=6][B]EzTax[/B][/SIZE]
[SIZE=3]Modern, configurable tax system for Vault-based Minecraft economies[/SIZE]
[SIZE=2]Paper 26.1+ • Java 25+ • Vault required • Optional: [URL='https://modrinth.com/plugin/ezeconomy']EzEconomy[/URL], EzSeasons[/SIZE][/CENTER]

Get support through [URL='https://discord.gg/yWP95XfmBS']the EzPlugins Discord server[/URL].

[SIZE=4][B]Why EzTax?[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Transaction Taxes[/B] – Deducts a configurable percentage or flat fee when a player sends money
[*][B]Wealth Tax[/B] – Periodically taxes players above a configurable balance threshold
[*][B]Progressive Tax[/B] – Realistic, tiered tax brackets (like real-world tax systems)
[*][B]Group-Based Taxation[/B] – Apply different tax rates based on player groups or permissions
[*][B]Player Exemptions[/B] – Exempt specific players from taxation
[*][B]Inactivity Fees[/B] – Tax players who have not been online for a set number of days
[*][B]Death Fees[/B] – Charge a percentage of balance on player death (configurable worlds/exemptions)
[*][B]Tax Sinks[/B] – Route collected taxes to a server account or remove them from the economy
[*][B]Tax History[/B] – Paginated, per-player transaction history via /tax history
[*][B]Stats & Sinks[/B] – View total tax collected and breakdown by type with /tax stats and /tax sinks
[*][B]In-Game Config GUI[/B] – Edit tax settings live from an inventory GUI
[*][B]Reloadable Config[/B] – Apply config changes instantly with /tax reload
[*][B]EzSeasons Integration[/B] – Logs a season-reset summary of dates and totals collected
[*][B]Full Vault Compatibility[/B] – Works with any Vault-based economy; [URL='https://modrinth.com/plugin/ezeconomy']EzEconomy[/URL] recommended
[*][B]MySQL Support[/B] – Store statistics and tax history in MySQL with automatic schema migration
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Requirements[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Java 25 or newer
[*]Paper 26.1+ (Minecraft 26.1)
[*]Vault
[*]A Vault-compatible economy plugin ([URL='https://modrinth.com/plugin/ezeconomy']EzEconomy[/URL] recommended)
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Commands[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]/tax check[/B] – Preview your next tax amounts and outstanding fines
[*][B]/tax pay[/B] – Pay all outstanding taxes immediately
[*][B]/tax payfine[/B] – Pay all outstanding fines immediately
[*][B]/tax show[/B] – Detailed breakdown of your current taxes
[*][B]/tax fines[/B] – Detailed breakdown of your outstanding fines
[*][B]/tax stats[/B] – View server-wide tax statistics
[*][B]/tax history [player][/B] – View paginated tax transaction history
[*][B]/tax reload[/B] – Reload config (admin)
[*][B]/tax sinks[/B] – View configured tax sink destinations (admin)
[*][B]/tax runwealthtax[/B] – Manually trigger a wealth tax cycle (admin/testing)
[*][B]/tax runtaxpayment[/B] – Manually trigger a tax payment run (admin/testing)
[*][B]/tax exempt/unexempt/exemptions[/B] – Manage player exemptions (admin)
[*][B]/tax settaxrate[/B] – Set global or group tax rates (admin)
[*][B]/tax transactiontax[/B] – View or manage transaction tax settings (admin)
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]Configuration Highlights[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*]Enable/disable each tax type independently
[*]Flat or progressive brackets for transaction tax and wealth tax
[*]Group-based rates and per-player exemptions
[*]Wealth tax threshold, percentage, and schedule interval
[*]Inactivity fee: days threshold, flat fee, percentage
[*]Death fee: percentage, maximum loss, world exemptions
[*]Tax sink: route to a named player account or remove from economy
[*]MySQL storage: host, port, database, table prefix
[*]Debug mode for troubleshooting
[/LIST]

[SIZE=4][B]FAQ[/B][/SIZE]
[LIST]
[*][B]Q: Does EzTax require EzEconomy?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A: No, but EzEconomy is recommended for best compatibility and features.
[/LIST]
[*][B]Q: Can I disable certain taxes?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A: Yes, each tax type is independently configurable in config.yml.
[/LIST]
[*][B]Q: Where do taxes go?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A: Taxes can be routed to a server account or simply removed from the economy (sink).
[/LIST]
[*][B]Q: Does it support MySQL?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A: Yes, EzTax can store statistics and tax history in MySQL with automatic schema migration.
[/LIST]
[*][B]Q: What changed in 2.0.0?[/B]
[LIST]
[*]A: Requires Minecraft 26.1 and Java 25. Adds Jaloquent ORM storage, tax history, EzSeasons integration, and an in-game config GUI.
[/LIST]
[/LIST]
